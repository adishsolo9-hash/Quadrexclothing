SHIPPING

Standard delivery across India takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery is available in metro cities within 1–2 business days.

Yes — international shipping is available at checkout for select regions, with delivery times of 7–14 business days depending on destination.

Shipping is free on all domestic orders above ₹4,999. Orders below that threshold carry a flat ₹99 shipping fee.

RETURNS

We offer 14-day returns on unworn items with original tags attached. Exchanges are free and processed as soon as we receive your item.

Head to your account, select the order, and choose "Start Return." You'll get a prepaid shipping label within 24 hours.

SIZING

All pieces are designed with an intentional oversized fit. If you prefer a closer fit, we recommend sizing down. Full measurements are on every product page.

Every product page includes a size guide with chest and length measurements in inches, based on the specific silhouette.

ORDERS

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placing them. After that, our fulfillment team begins processing and changes aren't guaranteed.

You'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS once your order ships. You can also track it from your account dashboard.

PAYMENTS

We accept UPI, all major credit and debit cards, net banking, and Cash on Delivery on eligible orders.

Yes — all transactions are processed through PCI-DSS compliant, encrypted payment gateways. We never store your card details.
