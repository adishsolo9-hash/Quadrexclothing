PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit QUADREX, browse products, or place an order, we collect information you provide directly (name, email, shipping address, payment details) and information collected automatically (device type, browser, pages visited, referring site).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support responses
  • To send marketing communications, only if you've opted in via the newsletter
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to run our business — payment processors, shipping carriers, and analytics tools — each bound by their own confidentiality obligations.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart contents, remember your wishlist, and understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some features (like the cart) rely on them to function.

5. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@quadrex.co.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to support@quadrex.co or via our Contact page.
